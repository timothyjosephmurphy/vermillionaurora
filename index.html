About the artist

TJ Murphy

TJ Murphy is a Seattle-based artist working in watercolor, watercolor pastel, and outdoor acrylic murals. Since 2020, he has explored the changing light of the Pacific Northwest and the expressions that give a portrait its character. His work brings luminous color, rich texture, and close observation to landscapes, portraits, and public spaces.
