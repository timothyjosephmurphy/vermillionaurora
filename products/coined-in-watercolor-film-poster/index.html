I made this poster for the premiere of Gavin Robertson’s documentary, Coined in Watercolor, at the Bitcoin Film Festival in Poland. It is based on a frame from the documentary showing me meditating on top of Table Mountain in Cape Town, South Africa.

I gave the painting as a gift to the organizer of the Bitcoin Film Festival.
