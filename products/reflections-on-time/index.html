Illustrated poetry book

Reflections on Time

By Paul Michael Murphy, illustrated by Timothy Joseph Murphy

This book brings together illustrations for twelve of my father’s poems. Each poem also has a connected song, sung by Suno, extending the work from the page into sound.

The project is a collaboration across generations: my father’s poems provide the source material, while my paintings respond to their images, moods, and movement.
