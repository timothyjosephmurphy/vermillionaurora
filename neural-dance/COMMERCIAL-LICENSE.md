This page summarizes how venues and partners can ask for a paid license. It grants no rights, sets no price, and is not a contract. Have a lawyer review any written license before you rely on it.

## Written license

A written license from TJ Murphy is required before any use, copying, modification, or distribution of Neural Dance. That includes running it at a venue, club, festival, gallery, or paid event, shipping it in a product, and making a commercial derivative work.

Contact [tj@tjm.art](mailto:tj@tjm.art).

## Source code

Source is shared only under a written agreement. A booking conversation, a quote, or access to the repository is not that agreement.

## Tiers

Each license is quoted by email. Prices are not listed here.

### Per venue or per event

One written license for a single venue, or for a single event, for the place and dates named in the agreement. An event includes a club night, a festival booking, a gallery showing, or a similar engagement. The agreement covers operating Neural Dance for that venue or event. Other venues, later dates, and products are included only when the agreement says so.

### Annual

One written license for a year of commercial operation by the organization named in the agreement, for the uses named there. That can be a venue's regular programming or a partner's ongoing installation. The term ends on the date the agreement states.

## What the agreement names

The written agreement names the tier, the venue, event, or organization, the dates, and whether a copy of the source is included. When source is included, it is shared only under that agreement.
