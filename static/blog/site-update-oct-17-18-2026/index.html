Site update: Oct 17–18

By TJ Murphy · October 18, 2026

I'm TJ Murphy, a painter based in Seattle. My studio is Vermillion Aurora. Here is what changed on the site in the two days after my weekly recap.

  • Oct 17. Sales tax at checkout now uses the Washington Department of Revenue rate lookup, and a failed lookup never blocks payment. Unframed prints get a half-inch border. Product pages show live sold or unavailable status. Published "My Week in Art (Oct 11–17)." Added a photo beside my bio on the About page. In tjm.art/play: a share card, a notice for in-app browsers, new tools, easter eggs, a Buy Scanner, sprinting and footsteps.
  • Oct 18. Cleared a batch of audit fixes: faster Buy buttons, lighter share images on product pages, internal catalog notes hidden, no Buy buttons on sold or not-for-sale work, and redirects for /shop and /checkout. Bitcoin checkout turned on for all available work. Homepage hero copy says originals and prints are for sale. Updated my education on About to match LinkedIn. In tjm.art/play: gate rooms between the museums, smoother transitions, holograms playing again, and more detailed models.
