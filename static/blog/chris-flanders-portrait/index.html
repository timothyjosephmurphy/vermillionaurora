Chris Flanders on His Portrait

By TJ Murphy · November 9, 2026

Chris Flanders owns a portrait I painted of him. He recorded a short video about living with it. Here he is, in his own words.

This beautiful work of art is a portrait TJ painted of me. I love it. It's an excellent likeness and has TJ's unique style and artistic expression, and I love having a one-of-a-kind original in my living room.

Thank you, Chris. It means a lot that this painting lives with you.
